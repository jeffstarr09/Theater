    House rules

    Every film screens once. Everyone watches the same frame at the same second — no pause, no scrub, no replay, no catching up later.

    Drop a film into an empty frame in the window, or buy a seat at the box office. Either way you walk in and sit down. When the house is ready the doors close and a public countdown starts.

    Afterwards the audience votes Best Picture. The winner is kept forever in the Hall of Fame; every other print is struck and survives only as a statistic.

    The House Manager sizes each theater to real demand — a quiet night gets a small room — and there is a guaranteed showtime every day regardless.
