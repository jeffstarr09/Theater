Filmmaker ticket

A filmmaker's seat is free — the price is a film. Fifteen to sixty seconds, mp4 or webm, under 50MB. It goes to the moderation desk, and if it is approved it takes a slot in the next programme.

If we turn it down you get a comped audience ticket to the next theater, automatically. If it is approved but the reel is already full, it rolls to the house after.

    What happens next

    Approved films join the running order for the house that is currently filling. When it fills — or when the guaranteed showtime arrives, whichever comes first — the doors close and a public countdown starts.

    At showtime everyone watches your film at the same second. They can throw roses and tomatoes at it. Afterwards they vote. One film is kept; the rest are struck.
