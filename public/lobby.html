    House rules

    Every film screens once. Everyone watches the same frame at the same second — no pause, no scrub, no replay, no catching up later.

    When the house is ready the doors close and a public countdown starts. At showtime the reel rolls. Afterwards the audience votes Best Picture; the winner is kept forever in the Hall of Fame and everything else survives only as a statistic.

    The House Manager sizes each theater to real demand. If the house is quiet it builds a smaller room — and there is a guaranteed showtime every night regardless.
